Our Story

Tradition Meets Modernity

Founded in the heart of Gaborone, Golden Dragon was born from a simple vision: to bring the authentic, fiery breath of the wok (Wok Hei) to Botswana.

Located in the iconic iTowers, just steps away from Room50Two, we blend centuries-old Chinese culinary techniques with the vibrant, modern energy of Botswana's capital.

The Master Chef

Our head chef brings over 20 years of experience from the bustling streets of Guangzhou. Every dish is tossed to perfection over an open flame, ensuring that every bite carries the smoky, savory essence that defines true Chinese cuisine.
